# my portfolio

a place to learn more about me: my work, projects, and what i care about. live at [vanessa-ng.vercel.app](https://vanessa-ng.vercel.app).

## what & why

- **the wheel.** i didn't want my portfolio to read like a résumé you scroll past. a wheel lets you wander, landing on whatever catches your eye first, the way you'd actually get to know someone. it also keeps everything on one screen, so no part of my story is buried at the bottom of a page.
- **every section looks different.** experience, projects, awards and the rest each have their own layout, because they're different kinds of things.
- **plain html, css and javascript.** no framework and no build step, so it's fast, simple, and free to host.
- **my own photos.** taken on my travels and around campus.
- **a journal anyone can read, only i can write.** entries live in `journal.md`. i edit it on github, and the site updates on its own.

## how it runs

pushes to `main` deploy automatically on vercel. content lives at the top of `script.js`, and the photos are in `images/`.
